Privacy

Your work stays yours.

Fluence and the Fluence Scan iPhone app collect no personal data. Here is exactly what touches the network, and why.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The short version

Fluence and Fluence Scan are made by Luminary Ops. Neither has accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Your projects, media, scans, and photos stay on your own computers and your own network. We don't collect, sell, or share personal data.

Fluence Scan (iPhone)

Fluence Scan collects no data.

  • Camera: used to read the pairing code Fluence shows and to photograph the scan patterns your projector displays.
  • Local network: used to connect to Fluence on your computer. The photos travel straight from your phone to that computer over your Wi-Fi or hotspot, and nowhere else.
  • No account or sign-in, no analytics, no third-party code. Nothing is sent to Luminary Ops or anyone else.

Fluence (Mac and Windows)

Fluence has no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising. It contacts two services:

  • License activation: when you activate a license, Fluence sends your license key and your computer's name to Lemon Squeezy, our store and license provider, so you can see and manage your activations. Fluence checks the license with Lemon Squeezy again from time to time. Without a license, nothing is sent.
  • Update checks: Fluence asks GitHub for the latest release so it can tell you when an update is available. The request contains no personal information.

Everything else stays local. Network features you turn on, such as NDI, OSC, the Control API, web sources, and Fluence Scan pairing, only talk to the devices and pages you choose.

Purchases

Lemon Squeezy sells Fluence licenses as the merchant of record. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy handles your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card details.

This website

fluencelive.com uses no cookies of its own and no analytics. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes visits (including IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site. Fonts load from Google Fonts, the download page asks GitHub for the latest release, and the checkout opens Lemon Squeezy's store on the page.

Changes

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
