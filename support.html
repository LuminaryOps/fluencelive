Fluence Scan (iPhone and Android)

Fluence Scan turns your phone into the camera for Surface Scan, which maps your projector onto the room automatically.

What you need

  • The latest version of Fluence, running on a Mac or Windows PC.
  • An iPhone running iOS 17 or later, or an Android phone running Android 9 or later.
  • The phone and the computer on the same Wi-Fi network, or the computer joined to the phone's hotspot.

Scanning, step by step

  1. In Fluence, open Output → Advanced Output, select the screen your projector shows, click Scan Surface and choose Phone as the camera. Fluence shows a QR code.
  2. Open Fluence Scan and point it at the QR code to pair.
  3. Pick the shape closest to the projection and aim so it fills the frame. Fluence shows white while the camera locks.
  4. Keep the phone still (a tripod or a steady surface helps), then start the scan from the phone or from Fluence.
  5. Review the result in Fluence.

Troubleshooting

The phone won't connect.

  • Check that the phone and the computer are on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks and some venue Wi-Fi stop devices from seeing each other; join the computer to the phone's hotspot instead.
  • If your computer asks whether Fluence may accept incoming network connections, allow it.
  • On the iPhone, make sure Fluence Scan is switched on under Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

The camera doesn't open.

  • On iPhone, allow camera access under Settings → Fluence Scan → Camera.
  • On Android, allow it under Settings → Apps → Fluence Scan → Permissions → Camera.

The app says the projection looks dim.

  • Try a wider lens, move closer, or dim the room further.

The app says the projection is too bright.

  • Lower the projector's brightness, or try another lens.

The connection dropped mid-scan.

  • Fluence Scan goes back to the pairing screen. Scan the code in Fluence again to reconnect, then restart the scan.

Fluence Scan has no account, analytics, or ads, and sends its photos only to Fluence on your own network. Details are in the privacy policy.

Still stuck? Email admin@fluencelive.com with your Fluence version, your computer, and your phone model.
